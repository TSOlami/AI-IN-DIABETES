\section{Data Modalities and Problem Formulation}

AI systems for diabetes care draw on several data types, each with distinct strengths and limitations.

\subsection{Wearable Sensors and Continuous Glucose Monitoring}

CGM has transformed blood sugar tracking. Devices measure interstitial glucose every 1--15 minutes, enabling detection of dangerous highs and lows, prediction of trends, and insulin fine-tuning \cite{alhaddad2022sense, contreras2018artificial, jacobs2023artificial}. Smartwatches and fitness trackers capture heart rate, activity, and sleep, which influence glucose regulation \cite{alhaddad2022sense, rodriguez2023applications}. Combining CGM with activity data allows models to adjust forecasts when someone has exercised or slept poorly \cite{contreras2018artificial, alhaddad2022sense}. Heart-rate-based glucose estimation from wearables has not yet proven accurate enough for clinical use \cite{zanelli2022diabetes, alhaddad2022sense}.

Wearable data pose challenges: devices differ in sampling rates and measurement techniques, hindering cross-brand models \cite{prioleau2025deep, langarica2024deep}; sensor dropout and gaps distort training and predictions \cite{xie2024processing, hirsch2024interruption}; and calibration drift alters readings over time \cite{alhaddad2022sense, jacobs2023artificial}. Processing these high-frequency, noisy signals requires architectures like LSTMs and substantial compute and data \cite{zhu2021deep, alam2024machine, martinsson2020blood}.

\subsection{Retinal Imaging Modalities}

Diabetic retinopathy affects about 35\% of patients and is a leading cause of blindness in working-age adults \cite{yau2012global}; early screening can prevent vision loss but many patients lack access. Fundus photography and OCT are the main screening and diagnostic techniques \cite{contreras2018artificial}.

AI-based retinal screening has achieved remarkable success in controlled settings. Fundus cameras capture color images of the back of the eye, revealing the tiny bleeds, protein deposits, and abnormal blood vessels that signal retinopathy \cite{selvachandran2022developments, abushawish2024deep}. A systematic review of regulator-approved deep learning systems found pooled sensitivity of 93\% and specificity of 90\%, performance that matches or exceeds trained human graders \cite{zhang2025systematic, liu2023prospective, wu2023efficacy}. OCT adds three-dimensional detail by imaging the retinal layers, helping clinicians assess swelling and structural damage \cite{contreras2018artificial, padhy2024systematic}.

Real-world deployment remains difficult. Image quality varies with camera, operator, and patient factors; 3--12\% of images are ungradable (higher for non-mydriatic: 12.1\% vs mydriatic: 3.4\%) \cite{zhang2025systematic, kanclerz2021imaging}. Models trained on research-grade images struggle with portable-camera quality in community settings \cite{raj2024federated, zhang2025systematic}. Most training data come from high-income countries \cite{wang2024ai, olusanya2024mitigating}, and label noise arises from grader disagreement on borderline cases \cite{zhang2025systematic}.

\subsection{Electronic Health Records and Clinical Data}

EHRs contain diagnoses, lab results, medications, vital signs, and notes \cite{contreras2018artificial, oikonomou2023machine}, enabling risk identification and earlier intervention \cite{wang2024ai}; NLP can extract structure from free text \cite{contreras2018artificial}. Challenges include high dimensionality, missing and irregular data \cite{oikonomou2023machine}, selection bias toward sicker patients with more complete records \cite{jacobs2023artificial}, and complex interactions that push toward deep learning \cite{zhu2021deep}.

\subsection{Problem Formulation and Modeling Objectives}

Researchers apply AI to diabetes care for several distinct purposes, each requiring different data, methods, and success metrics:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Glucose prediction:} Forecasting where blood sugar will be 30 to 60 minutes ahead, giving patients time to eat or take insulin before problems develop \cite{jacobs2023artificial, prioleau2025deep}.
    \item \textbf{Retinopathy screening:} Automatically grading eye images to determine which patients need specialist referral, enabling screening at scale \cite{zhang2025systematic, zhu2021deep}.
    \item \textbf{Risk prediction:} Estimating the likelihood that someone will develop diabetes or experience a complication like kidney disease or amputation \cite{oikonomou2023machine, wang2024ai}.
    \item \textbf{Treatment optimization:} Recommending personalized insulin doses, medication adjustments, or lifestyle changes \cite{mackenzie2023diabetes, contreras2018artificial}.
\end{itemize}

Each task requires careful choice of outcomes, prediction horizons, and clinical metrics \cite{jacobs2023artificial}. Imbalanced complications can make standard accuracy misleading \cite{alam2024machine}, and the progressive nature of diabetes demands modeling change over time \cite{zhu2021deep}.
